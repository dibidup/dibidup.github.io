Free app · Groove Player

Scan the book, play with the band

  • Each QR code in the book adds its grooves to your phone, with the notation and the sound of a real drum kit.
  • Slow the tempo down or speed it up without changing the sound, with metronome and count-in.
  • Bass, keys, guitars and a melody that follow every groove, with a volume control for each instrument.
  • Turn your phone sideways and the music fills the screen.
